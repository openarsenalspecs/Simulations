# DesignFlux
**Open Engineering Intelligence for Design, Simulation, and Manufacturing**

## Overview

DesignFlux is an open specification for a modular engineering intelligence platform that connects design workflows, physics-based simulation, artificial intelligence assistance, manufacturing preparation, knowledge management, and supply chain intelligence into a unified ecosystem.

The specification provides a foundation for engineers, researchers, manufacturers, educators, and innovators to move from early concepts through simulation, optimization, validation, and production planning. By combining engineering expertise with intelligent automation and open standards, DesignFlux reduces fragmentation between disconnected tools while creating a collaborative environment for developing advanced technologies.

DesignFlux is designed as an extensible engineering operating system where specialized capabilities can be added through modules and plugins. The architecture supports aerospace, advanced manufacturing, energy systems, robotics, industrial development, research, and other engineering disciplines while maintaining flexibility for future technologies.

## Specification Goals

DesignFlux is designed to:

- Provide an open framework for advanced engineering workflows
- Connect design, simulation, manufacturing, and operational processes
- Enable AI-assisted engineering while maintaining human oversight
- Create reusable engineering modules and shared standards
- Improve collaboration between technical disciplines
- Preserve engineering knowledge and design history
- Reduce dependency on fragmented proprietary systems
- Support local, distributed, and organizational deployments
- Enable community-driven expansion through plugins

# Architecture

DesignFlux uses a layered modular architecture consisting of core engineering modules, intelligence layers, security foundations, and optional plugin modules.

Core modules provide the essential capabilities required for a complete DesignFlux implementation. These modules define the foundation for engineering workflows, data management, simulation, manufacturing, intelligence, security, and governance.

Optional plugin modules extend DesignFlux for specialized industries, advanced workflows, and application-specific requirements.

# Design Principles

## Modular by Default

Every capability is designed as an independent module that can be extended, replaced, or upgraded without redesigning the entire platform.

## Open Standards

DesignFlux promotes interoperability through documented interfaces, shared data structures, and transparent specifications.

## Human-Centered Engineering

AI assists engineers by improving analysis and efficiency while preserving human judgment and responsibility.

## Secure by Design

Engineering data, intellectual property, and collaborative workflows are protected through built-in security principles.

## Knowledge Preservation

DesignFlux captures engineering knowledge, decisions, and relationships to prevent loss of institutional expertise.

## Extensible Ecosystem

Organizations and developers can create additional modules and plugins for specialized applications.
