# LunaSphere

## The foundation for a civilization that reaches beyond Earth

LunaSphere is an open AGPL-3.0+ specification defining the engineering architecture required to transform the Moon from an uninhabited environment into humanity's first self-sustaining off-world civilization.

The specification provides a modular framework for simulating, designing, and developing every major stage of lunar expansion, beginning with Earth as the home base and progressing toward permanent habitation, industrial independence, lunar launch capability, and interplanetary expansion.

LunaSphere focuses on the complete lifecycle of civilization development, including life support systems, biodomes, atmospheric engineering, water generation, resource utilization, manufacturing, transportation, robotics, energy systems, rocket infrastructure, and electromagnetic mass driver networks.

---

# Vision

Humanity's first permanent civilization beyond Earth requires more than a habitat. It requires a complete ecosystem capable of supporting life, industry, growth, and exploration.

LunaSphere defines the roadmap for creating:

- Self-sustaining lunar habitats
- Closed-loop biological systems
- Local resource production
- Lunar manufacturing capabilities
- Autonomous construction systems
- Space transportation infrastructure
- Interplanetary expansion systems

The Moon becomes the first step toward a civilization that reaches beyond Earth.

---

# Specification Goals

LunaSphere is designed to:

- Create a complete digital simulation framework for lunar civilization development
- Define modular engineering systems for settlement growth
- Model physical constraints and resource requirements
- Enable collaboration between researchers, engineers, developers, and organizations
- Provide open standards for future lunar infrastructure
- Support long-term expansion beyond the Moon
