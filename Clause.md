## Overview

Clause is an open-source civic simulation platform that models real-world governance through laws, budgets, infrastructure, economics, and population behavior. Rather than constructing fictional cities, Clause allows users to select real municipalities and simulate how policy decisions influence long-term sustainability, resilience, and prosperity.

The platform combines municipal finance, legal systems, demographics, infrastructure, environmental factors, and artificial intelligence into a transparent simulation engine capable of modeling years or decades of change. Every decision has measurable consequences, allowing users to explore complex interactions before similar policies are implemented in the real world.

Clause is designed for educators, researchers, civic technologists, urban planners, developers, students, elected officials, and anyone interested in understanding how communities function as interconnected systems.
