# RegenBlueprint
## Intelligent Ecosystem Design

RegenBlueprint is an open AGPL-3.0+ specification for intelligent ecosystem design, providing a modular framework for transforming degraded landscapes into resilient, biodiverse, and self-sustaining ecosystems.

The specification combines ecological science, AI planning, soil engineering, hydrology, biodiversity restoration, simulation, monitoring, economics, and community collaboration into a unified restoration architecture.

RegenBlueprint enables individuals, communities, researchers, organizations, and governments to analyze land, design restoration strategies, simulate long-term ecosystem evolution, and continuously improve ecological outcomes through adaptive management.

---

# Vision

The goal of RegenBlueprint is to create an open standard for restoring landscapes through science-driven planning and collaborative stewardship.

The specification provides a framework for turning degraded environments into thriving ecosystems containing:

- Healthy living soils
- Regenerative water systems
- Diverse plant communities
- Pollinator habitats
- Wildlife corridors
- Sustainable food systems
- Resilient ecological infrastructure

RegenBlueprint is designed for deserts, degraded farmland, damaged ecosystems, restoration projects, urban environments, and large-scale ecological regeneration initiatives.

# System Architecture

RegenBlueprint follows a modular architecture:

1. Land Analysis
2. Environmental Modeling
3. Soil and Water Engineering
4. Ecosystem Design
5. AI Planning
6. Implementation Management
7. Monitoring and Validation
8. Long-Term Adaptation

Each module can operate independently while contributing to the complete ecosystem restoration framework.
