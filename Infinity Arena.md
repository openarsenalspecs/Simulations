# Infinity Arena
## No Limits. No Mercy.

Infinity Arena is an open-source, AGPL 3.0+ modular tag-team fighting game built around paired combat systems, deep character creation, fusion transformations, DNA-based fighters, and a fully community-driven creator ecosystem.

Players build custom male-and-female fighter pairs, design their combat styles, create combo systems, equip modular weapons, and evolve fighters through a genetic-style DNA system. Every system is designed to be fully moddable and expandable by the community.

The game is not just a fighting game—it is a combat creation platform.

---

# Core Features

## Pair-Based Combat System
- 2v2 synchronized fighter system
- Male + female paired teams
- Assist-based combat mechanics
- Shared synergy meter
- Combo extension through partner interactions
- Team-based ultimates

---

## Fusion Mode System
- Temporary full synchronization of both fighters
- Merged movesets and abilities
- Cinematic transformation sequences
- Multiple fusion types including elemental, cybernetic, spirit, chaos, and beast fusion
- Enhanced damage, speed, and visual effects during fusion states

---

## Character Creator System
- Full visual customization of fighters
- Body type selection and animation presets
- Face, hair, clothing, and accessory customization
- Aura and visual effect system
- Animated cosmetics and identity expressions

---

## Character DNA System
- Trait-based genetic fighter system
- Movement, combat, elemental, and weapon DNA layers
- Evolution through gameplay progression
- Mutation paths including corruption, ascension, cyber enhancement, and spirit awakening
- Fully modular fighter creation system

---

## Fighting Style System
- Rushdown, grappler, zoner, assassin, acrobat, summoner, counter, and hybrid archetypes
- Mix-and-match combat roles
- Style-based movement and combo behavior
- Customizable combat philosophy per fighter

---

## Modular Weapon System
- Swappable weapon modules per character
- Weapon types include swords, scythes, gauntlets, chains, drones, pistols, and energy weapons
- Weapon traits such as heavy, agile, arcane, toxic, charged, and resonant
- Weapons modify animations, range, combo routes, and assist mechanics

---

## Custom Combo Editor
- Visual combo timeline editor
- Node-based combo construction system
- Assist timing customization
- Fusion cancel integration
- Camera and cinematic control tools
- Shareable combo builds and training scenarios

---

## Creator Marketplace System
- Community-driven content distribution platform
- Fighters, stages, weapons, combos, AI profiles, cosmetics, announcers, and UI themes
- Version control and dependency tracking for mods
- AGPL-compatible fork and remix support
- Creator rating and discovery system

---

## Spectator and Esports System
- Cinematic camera system for matches
- Instant replay and slow-motion highlights
- Live combo and damage statistics
- AI-assisted match commentary
- Broadcast-ready overlays and tournament tools
- Replay export system for content creation

---

## Accessibility and Training Systems
- Fully customizable input system
- Simplified control modes for accessibility
- Frame data viewer for advanced players
- Hitbox and hurtbox visualization tools
- AI sparring partners and adaptive training bots
- Matchup training simulations
- Execution assist and learning tools

---

## Arena System
- Dynamic interactive battle stages
- Environmental hazards and stage events
- Collapsing terrain and destructible objects
- Weather and dimensional effects
- Stage-triggered combat modifiers

---

## AI System
- Adaptive enemy AI with personality traits
- Training AI that learns from player behavior
- Ghost fighters based on player performance
- AI-driven sparring and matchup simulation

---

## Online Multiplayer
- Rollback netcode architecture
- Ranked and casual matchmaking
- Tournament and faction-based competition modes
- Community server support
- Replay synchronization and validation systems

---

## Modding and SDK Support
- Fully open modding architecture
- Fighter creation SDK
- Stage and asset creation tools
- Combat system scripting support
- Community-driven balance and expansion support

# Game Philosophy

Infinity Arena is designed around three core principles:

- No Limits: deep customization across all systems
- No Mercy: competitive combat with high skill expression
- Infinite Creation: community-driven expansion of all content

Every fighter, system, and mechanic is designed to be modular, customizable, and expandable.

- Built with an open-source game engine such as Godot Engine
- Rollback netcode for competitive stability
- Data-driven character and combat system architecture
- Modular asset and plugin system for community expansion
