# The Evidence Constitution

A modular framework for constitutional logic and investigative systems.

---

## Overview

The Evidence Constitution is an open-source multiplayer investigative simulation platform where players reconstruct truth through structured reasoning under law, evidence, uncertainty, and cognitive constraints.

It blends:
- Constitutional interpretation systems
- Investigative reconstruction mechanics
- Multi-jurisdiction legal simulation
- Structured argumentation frameworks
- AI-generated visual environments tied to verified state
- Cognitive modeling (bias, attention, pressure)
- Modern civic identity simulation

Players do not simply “solve cases.”  
They build, test, and challenge structured reasoning models of reality.

---

## Core Design Philosophy

- Evidence is structured, not narrative-driven
- Law is modular and jurisdiction-based
- Truth is constructed through reasoning, not authority
- Outcomes are multi-state, not strictly binary
- Uncertainty is a core gameplay mechanic
- Human cognition is limited and modeled
- AI visuals reflect only verified system state
- Multiplayer collaboration is required for full reasoning coverage

---

## Multiplayer Structure

- **2–4 players per case instance (maximum 4)**
- Shared evidence graph system
- Synchronized AI-generated visual scenes
- Role-based reasoning perspectives

### Roles (Dynamic)
- Investigator (evidence discovery)
- Analyst (timeline + reconstruction)
- Legal Reasoner (constitutional/jurisdiction logic)
- Civic Journalist / Observer (external narrative pressure)

Roles influence perception, interpretation, and UI emphasis.
