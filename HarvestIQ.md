# HarvestIQ

**The Future of Farming, Today.**

HarvestIQ is an AI-powered land intelligence, simulation, and optimization platform that transforms raw land data into actionable, real-world strategies for maximum productivity, profitability, and sustainability. It allows landowners to simulate, design, and optimize every square inch of their property using AI-driven models that incorporate terrain, market conditions, zoning laws, and personal skill profiles.

---

## 🌱 Overview

HarvestIQ enables users to upload land surveys and combine them with personal goals, skills, and local economic conditions to generate optimized land-use plans. The system simulates multiple scenarios and ranks them based on profitability, sustainability, and feasibility.

The platform is fully modular, allowing independent AI systems to handle land mapping, economic forecasting, zoning compliance, and optimization logic.

---

## 🧩 Core Features

### 🗺️ Land Intelligence & Mapping
- Upload land surveys (GIS, CAD, image-based, PDF)
- Convert land data into interactive 2D/3D maps
- Terrain analysis (elevation, slope, drainage)
- Sunlight, shade, and seasonal exposure modeling
- Soil composition and fertility classification
- Water flow and irrigation mapping

---

### 🤖 AI Land Optimization Engine
- Micro-level land segmentation (down to square-foot optimization)
- Multi-objective optimization (profit, yield, sustainability, labor efficiency)
- Constraint-based planning engine
- Reinforcement learning for adaptive land strategies
- Scenario ranking and comparison system

---

### 🧾 Local Zoning Law Module
- Automated parsing of municipal zoning regulations
- Detection of permitted vs restricted land uses
- Setback, density, height, and usage constraint enforcement
- Zoning overlay visualization on interactive maps
- Compliance validation for all generated plans
- Variance simulation (“what would need to change to be approved”)
- Township/county-specific rule engine support

---

### 👤 Skill & Lifestyle Matching System
- User profile system (skills, tools, time availability)
- Goal-based optimization (profit, food production, sustainability, hybrid)
- Hobby integration (beekeeping, gardening, livestock, etc.)
- Labor requirement estimation
- Personalized land-use weighting system

---

### 📊 Market & Economic Intelligence
- Local crop and commodity price tracking
- Demand trend forecasting
- Revenue simulation per land configuration
- Cost estimation (labor, materials, infrastructure)
- ROI comparison between scenarios
- Risk-adjusted financial modeling

---

### 🎮 Simulation & Scenario Engine
- What-if scenario sandbox
- Multi-variable land strategy comparison
- Seasonal and multi-year simulations
- Risk modeling (weather, pests, price volatility)
- Scenario branching and replay system
- Optimization scoring system

---

### 🌿 Sustainability & Environmental Scoring
- Carbon impact estimation
- Soil health and regeneration tracking
- Water efficiency optimization
- Biodiversity and ecosystem scoring
- Permaculture design evaluation
- Long-term ecological forecasting

---

### 🧾 Action Planning System
- Step-by-step execution roadmaps
- Seasonal planting and harvesting schedules
- Infrastructure planning (irrigation, fencing, energy systems)
- Cost and materials breakdown
- Exportable implementation plans (PDF/GIS overlays)
- Construction-ready zoning-compliant blueprints

---

### 🧠 AI Assistant Layer
- Conversational land-use planning assistant
- Natural language queries (“best use for 2 acres in NJ?”)
- Explainable AI decisions
- Real-time plan adjustments
- Continuous learning from user feedback

---

### 🎮 Gamification System
- Profit, efficiency, and sustainability scoring
- Achievement system for optimized land use
- Challenge modes (budget, climate constraints, etc.)
- Scenario benchmarking
- Progress tracking over seasons and years

---

### 🔐 Platform & Infrastructure
- Fully modular architecture
- Plugin-based AI system design
- Secure encrypted data storage
- API-first backend design
- Scalable cloud deployment (Docker + Kubernetes)
- External dataset and model integration support

## 🧱 Architecture Philosophy

HarvestIQ is built as a **modular AI ecosystem**, where each subsystem operates independently but communicates through a unified orchestration layer:

- Land Mapping Engine  
- Optimization Engine  
- Zoning Compliance Engine  
- Market Intelligence Engine  
- Simulation Engine  
- AI Assistant Layer  

Each module can be replaced, extended, or improved without breaking the system.

---

## 💻 Open Source Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js (React) + Mapbox / Leaflet (GIS visualization)
- **Backend:** Python + FastAPI
- **AI/ML:**  
  - Reinforcement learning models  
  - Constraint optimization solvers  
  - Predictive market analytics  
- **Geospatial:** PostGIS, GeoPandas, Shapely  
- **Data Processing:** Pandas, NumPy, Dask  
- **Orchestration:** LangChain or custom agent framework  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL + PostGIS  
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes  
- **Real-time Systems:** WebSockets / event-driven architecture
