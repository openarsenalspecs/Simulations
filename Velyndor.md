# Velyndor – Skills, Growth, Legacy

## Overview

Velyndor is a next-generation open source MMO designed around **adventure, personal growth, professional development, and player-driven world creation**. Released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, Velyndor provides a modular foundation for building a living, evolving universe where players explore, fight, craft, build, and leave a lasting legacy.

Velyndor combines deep character specialization, AI-powered world generation, sustainable crafting systems, dynamic economies, and interactive AI assistance to create an MMO experience where every player decision influences the world.

The goal of Velyndor is to create a transparent, community-developed virtual world where technology, creativity, and player collaboration continuously expand the game.

---

# Core Vision

Velyndor is built around five core principles:

- **Player Choice** – Every character develops through meaningful skill decisions.
- **Living World** – The environment, economy, and stories evolve based on player activity.
- **AI-Assisted Evolution** – Artificial intelligence helps generate content, balance gameplay, and support players.
- **Sustainable Development** – Crafting and construction systems encourage ecological balance.
- **Open Source Collaboration** – Developers, artists, designers, and players contribute to a shared universe.
